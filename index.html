Travel Companion
In development

Plan the whole trip in one place.

Travel Companion is a trip planner we are building for travellers who want clear choices instead of endless lists: a checklist of what to sort, flight and hotel options, a map, and a day-by-day itinerary.

A checklist that fits your trip

Flights, stay, visas, insurance, an eSIM and more, built from your destination, dates and travellers.

Hotel and flight options

A short, curated set of options with the details that matter, then a link to our booking partners to finish the booking.

A map and an itinerary

See hotels on a map and plan your days around the places you actually want to visit.

Affiliate disclosure. Some links on Travel Companion go to booking partners. If you book through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. We do not sell travel ourselves and bookings are completed on the partner's website.
